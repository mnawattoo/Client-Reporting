The Starfish & Coffee Spot: a charming beach house steps from the sand.

Escape to this charming 3-bedroom beach home in the tranquil Laguna Beach neighborhood, just steps from the sand and surf.

  • Sleeps 9: 2 king beds, 2 queen beds and 1 full bed
  • Free on-site parking; golf cart rentals nearby
